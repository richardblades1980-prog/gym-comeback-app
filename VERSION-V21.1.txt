GYM COMEBACK
Version: V21.1B.1
Release: Beta testing and workout logging fixes
Base checkpoint: V21.1A.2 — Objective-to-Plan Integration

Changes in this release:
- Workout completion status is scoped to a workout session and starts fresh after a saved session, while historical completion records remain available for workout-day unlocking.
- Timed exercises provide a per-set stopwatch with start, stop and reset controls.
- Target duration is displayed separately from actual elapsed time.
- Actual duration can be entered manually or corrected after timing, and may be shorter or longer than the target.
- Completed exercises no longer collapse automatically; the user chooses when to collapse them after recording results.
- Adds a separate two-step Factory Reset — Start as a New User action.
- Existing Reset all progress remains separate and retains profile, equipment and programme setup.

Beta status: Requires user testing on deployed hosting and target devices.

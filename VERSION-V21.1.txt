GYM COMEBACK
Version: V21.1B.2
Release: Selectable timed repetition recording
Base checkpoint: V21.1B.1 — Beta testing and workout logging fixes

Changes in this release:
- Adds per-exercise recording modes: Normal repetitions, Timed hold, and Timed repetitions.
- Recording mode is independent of progression preference; choosing Weight first does not remove the selected timer.
- Timed repetitions record weight where applicable, actual elapsed time and completed repetitions for each set.
- Timed holds retain stopwatch and actual-duration logging without a reps field.
- Existing duration-first exercises migrate to Timed hold; other exercises default to Normal repetitions.
- Timed repetition progression review treats a set as fully recorded only when both time and reps are present, without blocking workout saving.

Preserves all V21.1B.1 fixes and V21.1A.2 objective-to-plan integration.

Beta status: Requires user testing on deployed hosting and target devices.

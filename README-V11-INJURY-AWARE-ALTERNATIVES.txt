GYM COMEBACK V11 — INJURY-AWARE ALTERNATIVES

Built from the beta-tested V10.1 package. V10.1 remains the passed backup and is not overwritten.

What's new
- Workout cards now use a “Safer swap” button.
- Substitute suggestions consider the selected pain/restriction area, target muscles, and equipment availability.
- Movements that may load the selected area are pushed down the list and flagged for extra caution.
- If pain is reported as 7/10 or more, or the restriction is multiple/unspecified, the app prompts the user to pause and consider professional advice before viewing alternatives.
- The coach explains the limits of the suggestions and reinforces symptom-led stop rules.
- The persistent version badge, About section, and service-worker cache identify V11.

Retained from V10.1
- Smart progression, symptom response logging, five workout days, custom equipment, gear-matched workouts, exercise library, muscle/equipment visual guides, progress dashboard, CSV export, and JSON backup.

Beta test checklist
1. Confirm VERSION V11 stays in the top-right while navigating and scrolling.
2. Open Coach and save a test profile with “No current injury / restriction” and pain 0; open a workout and press Safer swap. Confirm a muscle-matched list appears and equipment availability is shown.
3. Change profile area to Foot / ankle and try Safer swap on a leg or impact exercise. Confirm the list identifies potentially aggravating options with a caution marker.
4. Set pain to 7 and try Safer swap. Confirm the extra confirmation appears; cancel it and check the app remains responsive.
5. Choose an alternative, confirm the workout updates, and confirm logs can still be entered/saved.
6. Check V10 smart progression still reacts to “Worse / aggravated” by advising to hold/reduce rather than progress.
7. Check equipment matching, muscle guide, five days, progress, CSV export, and JSON backup/restore.
8. Test on phone. If installed as a PWA, close/reopen or refresh once so the new service-worker cache can update.

Safety note
These are rule-based candidates only, not medical advice or a guarantee that a movement is safe for a particular diagnosis. Stop movements that cause sharp or worsening symptoms and follow a clinician's restrictions. Severe/new symptoms need qualified medical assessment.

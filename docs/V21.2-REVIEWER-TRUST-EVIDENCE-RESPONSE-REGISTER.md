## 1. Purpose

This register tracks the evidence and advice required before a final reviewer-trust architecture decision can be responsibly prepared. It converts the companion input brief into individually traceable questions, required evidence, competent input, decision authority, dependencies and status.

It is a blank control register, not evidence that advice has been obtained. No item may be marked received, assessed or closed without a source or documented response and an identified reviewer of that evidence.

**Initial state:** all entries are **OPEN — NOT YET EVIDENCED**. Where a decision depends on missing professional, governance or legal input, it is also a **BLOCKER** for that downstream decision. No response, source, adviser or approval is presumed.

## 2. Status definitions

| Status | Meaning | Permitted transition |
|---|---|---|
| OPEN — NOT YET EVIDENCED | No adequate response/evidence is recorded | Evidence may be received through a separately authorised process |
| BLOCKED — PREREQUISITE MISSING | A material dependency is unresolved or advice is unavailable | Remains blocked until the prerequisite is evidenced and assessed |
| EVIDENCE RECEIVED — NOT ASSESSED | A response/source exists but has not been checked for scope, authority, currency or completeness | Independent/owner assessment required |
| UNDER REVIEW | Evidence is being assessed; no decision is implied | Record findings, limitations and disagreement |
| CONFLICT / CLARIFICATION REQUIRED | Sources conflict, are ambiguous, stale or incomplete | Obtain clarification or retain blocker |
| OWNER DECISION REQUIRED | Required expert assessment is complete enough to present a bounded decision | Owner may decide only within their authority |
| CLOSED — ACCEPTED FOR STATED PURPOSE | Evidence and decision are accepted by the correct authority for the recorded purpose/version | Reopen on trigger, change, expiry or contradictory evidence |

A status change must include a date, rationale, evidence reference and person/role authorised to make that change. A row must never be closed merely because a related document exists or a governance framework has passed.

## 3. Response record fields

For each item, maintain the following fields in the evidence record. The table below is a blank schema; do not populate it with personal data in this repository.

| Field | Required content |
|---|---|
| Item ID | Stable ID from this register; do not recycle IDs |
| Question and scope | Exact question answered and the product/content scope covered |
| Input authority | Relevant professional discipline, governance authority, legal adviser or independent security assessor |
| Authority/scope check | Why the respondent/source is competent and authorised for this question; avoid unnecessary personal details |
| Response | Concise answer, conclusion and rationale |
| Conclusion class | Legal/professional requirement, authoritative fact, recommendation, design option, assumption or unresolved |
| Evidence/source | Official source, standard, version, publication/effective date, assessment report or documented response |
| Jurisdiction and assumptions | Australia and any narrower legal, organisational, technical or product assumptions |
| Limitations and confidence | Gaps, uncertainty, contrary evidence, confidence and what is not established |
| Dependencies | Other item IDs that must be resolved first |
| Assessed by | Role/authority that reviewed the evidence |
| Decision authority | Who may accept the answer or make the downstream decision |
| Status | One of the controlled values in section 2 |
| Last reviewed / next trigger | Date and event requiring reassessment |
| Decision reference | Link to an approved decision record, if one exists |

Do not store identity documents, credential scans, private contact details, signatures, conflict declarations, complaint files, investigation material, health information or other sensitive personal information in this register or ordinary repository documentation. Use only appropriately approved, non-sensitive evidence references. Any restricted evidence process requires separate legal/privacy, security and owner approval.

## 9. Evidence quality and closure rules

An item may move to **EVIDENCE RECEIVED — NOT ASSESSED** only when a concrete response or source is recorded with its date, version, jurisdiction and scope.

It may move to **UNDER REVIEW** only when an identified assessor has accepted responsibility to check:
- respondent/source authority and competence for the question;
- currency, provenance and completeness;
- applicability to the actual product, entity, jurisdiction and intended use;
- assumptions, limitations and contrary evidence;
- dependencies and conflicts with other advice.

Use **CONFLICT / CLARIFICATION REQUIRED** when two credible sources disagree, the answer is ambiguous, the source is stale, or the respondent is outside scope. Do not silently choose the more convenient answer.

Use **OWNER DECISION REQUIRED** only after the necessary professional/legal/security assessment is complete enough to frame a bounded decision. The owner may decide product risk and trade-offs, but must not override missing specialist approval or make legal/professional determinations outside their competence.

Use **CLOSED — ACCEPTED FOR STATED PURPOSE** only with a linked decision, named decision authority, exact purpose/version, date and any conditions. Closure must be reopened when the law, standards, professional status, product scope, threat model, provider, architecture, content claims or operating assumptions materially change.

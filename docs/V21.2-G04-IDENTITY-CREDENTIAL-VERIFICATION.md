# V21.2 — G-04 identity and credential verification process

**Status:** DESIGN PROPOSAL — owner decision required. This document proposes a verification process only. It does not authorise collection of identity documents or credentials, create a reviewer register, appoint reviewers or approve any credential pathway.

**Governance basis:** G-01 project-owner accountability, G-02 Australia-first jurisdiction and the owner-approved-in-principle G-03 scope-based qualification framework. The exact credential pathways remain to be established with appropriately qualified Australian professional input.

## 1. Decision sought

Approve the process principles and verification outcomes below as the method for independently checking a future reviewer's identity, qualifications, current standing and scope fit.

This is not approval of specific identity documents, credential types, verification providers, professional categories or legal bases for collecting personal information. Those choices require the G-03 qualification matrix, privacy/access/retention decision G-09 and any relevant legal advice.

## 2. Four separate verification questions

Do not collapse these into a single “verified” flag.

1. **Identity verified:** an independent verifier has established that the applicant is the person linked to the evidence being checked.
2. **Credential verified:** the claimed qualification, registration or other approved competence evidence is confirmed through an authoritative source or an approved alternative process.
3. **Current standing assessed:** status, expiry, restrictions, conditions, jurisdiction and relevant scope limitations have been checked and recorded.
4. **Scope fit accepted:** the verified evidence has been assessed against the approved G-03 competence criteria for the exact review scope and permitted tasks.

A credential may be genuine but expired, restricted, unrelated to the requested scope, or insufficient for the specific task. Verification of authenticity is not the same as a decision that it qualifies the person.

## 3. Independent verification hierarchy

Use the strongest available source appropriate to the evidence type:

1. **Statutory registration or licensing:** check directly with the responsible Australian registration/licensing authority using its authoritative public register or formal verification channel.
2. **Awarded qualification:** check directly with the awarding education institution or its authorised verification service.
3. **Professional certification or membership:** check directly with the issuing body, including the credential's current status and any relevant scope or conditions.
4. **Experience-based competence pathway:** only if expressly permitted by the final G-03 matrix, assess documented work history, references and a structured task-relevant assessment. Self-attestation alone is insufficient.
5. **No authoritative verification available:** mark as inconclusive/not verified unless a separately approved, documented alternative provides equivalent assurance. Do not quietly lower the standard.

A CV, social-media profile, marketing page, copied certificate, referee statement or applicant declaration may help identify a claim but does not independently prove current credential status. A supplied document should be corroborative, not the sole verification source where an authoritative source is available.

## 4. Identity matching

The eventual procedure must establish that the person whose identity is checked is the same person named in the authoritative credential or registration record.

- Use an independent identity-checking method proportionate to the risk and privacy impact.
- Confirm only the minimum identity attributes necessary to distinguish the person and link them to the credential record.
- Resolve name changes or reasonable record discrepancies through a documented, privacy-conscious process.
- Do not treat possession of a certificate, email address, online account or professional profile as identity proof.
- Do not let the applicant act as the sole verifier of their own identity or credentials.
- Do not retain full identity documents by default. Prefer recording the verification outcome, verifier, date and minimal reference needed for audit, subject to the approved privacy design.
- The accepted identity evidence, provider, storage and deletion method remain open until G-09 and relevant legal/privacy review are complete.

## 5. Verification record (restricted)

If and when collection is separately authorised, a restricted record should capture only what is needed to support the governance decision:

- internal pseudonymous reviewer reference;
- identity verification outcome and method category;
- qualification/registration category as approved under G-03;
- issuing or registering authority;
- authoritative source and exact record/reference locator where appropriate;
- date checked, verifier identity/role and verification result;
- status at time checked, expiry/review date and any relevant restrictions or conditions;
- jurisdiction and scope-of-practice assessment;
- mapping to permitted review scopes, tasks and explicit exclusions;
- unresolved discrepancies, escalation and decision rationale;
- next recheck date and event-triggered recheck requirements;
- appointment decision-maker, authorisation period and audit history.

Do not place identity documents, credential scans, private contact details, home addresses, full registration evidence or unnecessary personal information in Git, public catalogue assets, the application bundle, user backups or public release manifests.

Public catalogue traceability should use a pseudonymous reviewer ID and only the minimum scope/decision/freshness details later approved under G-12.

## 6. Verification outcomes and fail-closed rules

Use explicit outcomes rather than a boolean “verified” field:

| Outcome | Meaning | Governance treatment |
|---|---|---|
| Verified | Identity and relevant credential evidence confirmed; status and scope fit assessed | May proceed to separate appointment decision; not automatically authorised |
| Verified with limits | Evidence confirmed but has restrictions, conditions or bounded scope | No authority beyond the documented limits; requires explicit governance acceptance |
| Inconclusive | Authoritative source unavailable, evidence ambiguous, identity mismatch unresolved or material facts cannot be confirmed | Not eligible for appointment in the affected scope until resolved |
| Not verified | Claim contradicted, evidence invalid, identity not established or required evidence absent | No appointment for the affected scope |
| Expired / not current | Credential or required standing is no longer current | No new review authority until reverified and reauthorised |
| Suspended / revoked | Relevant authority or governance authorisation is suspended or revoked | Stop new reviews in affected scope; assess prior approvals under policy |

A failed or inconclusive check must not be converted into approval because the applicant is experienced, well-known, recommended or urgently needed.

## 7. Rechecks and change events

Recheck on the approved schedule and sooner when a material trigger occurs, including:

- credential or registration expiry;
- a change in registration status, restrictions or conditions;
- change of name or identity discrepancy;
- change in jurisdiction or requested review scope;
- credible complaint or concern relevant to competence or conduct;
- issuer or authority notification;
- evidence of credential fraud, impersonation or key compromise;
- a material change to G-03 criteria or the reviewer's permitted tasks.

The exact cadence and trigger handling must be consistent with the relevant authority and G-05 appointment/recheck policy. Do not invent a universal interval.

A lapsed or unverified status must suspend new review authority for the affected scope. Previously signed reviews are not silently rewritten; assess them under the approved withdrawal/revocation process and current release policy.

## 8. Separation of duties and audit

- The applicant supplies claims and cooperates; they do not determine their own verification result.
- A verifier checks source evidence; a separate authorised governance decision-maker assesses scope fit and appointment.
- A reviewer cannot verify or approve their own credential evidence.
- The project owner retains policy accountability but cannot substitute personal judgement for required professional qualification or verification.
- Sensitive verification evidence is accessible only to designated roles under a future approved access policy.
- Every check, result, correction and decision is auditable and append-only; corrections do not erase the original event.
- Where a conflict exists, the verifier or decision-maker must recuse and another authorised person must handle the check.

## 9. Privacy and legal prerequisites

No real identity or credential data may be collected until G-09 is approved and the safeguards are operational.

Before that point, the governance authority must determine:

- the lawful purpose and basis for each data item;
- what notice is given to applicants and how their information is used;
- which identity and credential data are strictly necessary;
- whether direct source checks can avoid retaining document copies;
- who may access, verify, correct and delete restricted records;
- secure storage, encryption, audit logging and breach response;
- retention and deletion periods, including how long evidence is needed to defend historical approval decisions;
- any third-party verification provider, data location or cross-border transfer;
- independent privacy/legal review where required.

Do not use public Git history, issue trackers, email threads or ordinary user backups as a credential repository.

## 10. Implementation boundaries

This proposal does not authorise:
- collection of any real identity or credential data;
- selection or connection of a verification provider;
- creation of a live reviewer register or reviewer account;
- reviewer appointment, key generation or signing infrastructure;
- production content review or approval;
- changes to catalogue schema, database, backup, service worker, runtime or UI;
- C3 or injury-aware matching implementation;
- changes to main, Pages release candidate, Pages settings or deployment.

A later technical gate must specify the restricted record system, access controls, audit, retention enforcement, verification evidence references, registry integration and tests only after G-03 final criteria, G-09 privacy decisions and explicit owner approval.

- Should identity verification be performed by a designated independent verifier or an approved specialist provider?
- Which alternative is acceptable when no authoritative register or issuer verification service exists?
- What minimum audit record can be retained without keeping identity-document copies?
- Who may perform verification, and who separately decides scope fit and appointment?
- What discrepancy or restriction requires automatic rejection versus escalation?
- What recheck triggers must immediately suspend new reviews?

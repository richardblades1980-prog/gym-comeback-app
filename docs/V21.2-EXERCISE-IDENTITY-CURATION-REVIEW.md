# V21.2 exercise identity curation review — draft recommendations

**Status:** Review proposal only. No identity mapping is approved by this document. No canonical IDs, aliases, variations or production catalogue records are created.
**Source baseline:** protected root `index.html`, blob `e3eb0093648cc6c5d706bb5b1008b58ec01a5576`; source register `docs/V21.2-GYM-EXERCISE-SOURCE-REGISTER.md`.
**Review scope:** the ten base-programme labels that do not exactly match an optional exercise-library row.

## Decision standard

A verified alias is a source-scoped, exact-string mapping to one exercise definition. It does not erase the source label or its historical snapshot. A variation is a separately identified execution difference under a reviewed parent definition. Similar muscle targets, equipment keywords or normalized names are not sufficient evidence by themselves.

The source's `baseExerciseReady()`, `machineDescriptions`, `equipmentAsset()` and optional-library gear-match fields are considered separately below. Gear-match text is equipment availability data, not an alias list.

## Recommended review outcome

- **Four proposed exact aliases for human approval:** Chest Press Machine, Leg Press, Seated Leg Extension and Shoulder Press Machine.
- **Two distinct position-specific variation candidates:** Seated Hamstring Curl and Prone or Lying Leg Curl. Their shared parent is not yet approved.
- **Three keep-separate / unresolved concepts:** Abdominal Crunch, Incline Chest Press and Dumbbell Biceps Curl relative to Cable Biceps Curl.
- **One catalogue addition candidate:** Dumbbell Lateral Raise.

These are recommendations, not verified mappings. The implementation must continue to leave every source label unresolved until an explicit review event records the decision. Do not generate stable IDs from these names.

## Implementation constraints

1. Preserve programme slot label, source namespace, source file/blob and source guide provenance.
2. If the four alias proposals are approved, record each exact base-programme label as its own source-scoped alias to a reviewed definition. Do not use normalized matching as the resolution key.
3. If the two leg-curl variations are approved, create separate variation IDs with explicit position attributes; do not treat them as interchangeable for history or progression.
4. Keep equipment requirements separate from identity. A machine's presence or a gear-match string is not proof of movement equivalence.
5. Keep unresolved candidates usable and visible. Do not invent placeholder definitions to make the review count appear complete.
6. Do not edit the protected root source, change historical records, alter the database/backup contract, publish a production catalogue or change the workout UI as part of this review.

## Source references inspected

- `baseWorkoutExercises` / `workouts`: the exact five-day programme labels, group keys and muscle-key snapshots.
- `baseExerciseReady()`: programme-specific equipment requirements; notably the abdominal crunch machine-or-mat alternative and incline press's multiple equipment paths.
- `machineDescriptions`: separate guide entries for the programme labels, including seated and prone/lying hamstring curls.
- `bodyExerciseLibrary`: the 42 optional-library rows and their exact labels/categories/muscle keys/art references.
- `equipmentAsset()`: illustration selection, treated as supporting UI provenance only, not identity proof.

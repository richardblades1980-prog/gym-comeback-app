# V21.2 artwork sample review — first concepts

**Status:** Review samples only. Neither image is production-approved, connected to the exercise catalogue, nor included in the verified guide cache.
**Visual basis:** current V21.2 dark UI language — navy surfaces, lime accent and blue highlights.
**Purpose:** prove the two most important artwork formats before creating a complete replacement collection.

| Artwork | File | Master canvas | Review purpose |

| Muscle illustration | `v21.2/assets/artwork-samples/v1/muscles/muscle-chest.svg` | SVG viewBox 1200 × 1600 (3:4) | Portrait anatomical figure with the pectoral area highlighted in the V21.2 lime accent |

The sample inventory is `v21.2/assets/artwork-samples/v1/manifest.json`. It has its own sample IDs and an explicit `review-only-not-for-production` status. These identifiers are not production catalogue or guide asset IDs.

## 2. Style direction being tested

- Dark navy / blue technical palette based on the V21.2 app.
- Lime highlight used sparingly for the exercise's primary focus.
- Transparent canvas; no baked-in captions, exercise names, labels or UI.
- Equipment art uses a clean, technical, three-quarter view and metallic material shading.
- Muscle art uses consistent body shading and a clearly separated focus muscle group.
- SVG master canvases remain scalable and can render responsively without stretching.

## 3. Important accuracy boundary

The chest press illustration is generic artwork only. It does not identify a real gym brand, machine model, linkage geometry, pin-selected configuration or plate-loaded configuration. It must not be used to decide the unresolved exercise-family / machine-variation mapping.

The chest muscle illustration is a simplified anatomical visual sample, not a medical reference. It needs visual and anatomy review before use in a published exercise guide. The guide text and the highlighted muscle group must be separately reviewed; the artwork alone does not establish exercise-specific instruction.

## 4. Review gates before a full batch

1. **Visual direction:** is the navy/blue/lime treatment suitable, and should the rendering be more photographic, comic-style or technical?
2. **Equipment fit:** is the level of detail, three-quarter view and machine depiction suitable for generic equipment illustration?
3. **Muscle presentation:** is the anatomy sufficiently clear, with the right amount of detail and a consistent portrait composition?
4. **Accessibility:** keep all essential meaning in adjacent text/accessible labels; never rely on colour alone to identify a muscle.
5. **Screen fit:** verify legibility on the V21.2 dark panel and narrow mobile layout before approving the full size set.

## 5. Release boundary and next step

These samples are intentionally outside `v21.2/assets/guides/`, the production guide manifest and `sw.js` cache routing. They do not change the existing 41-file immutable v1 guide package, offline behaviour, database, backup, historical data or legacy root app.

Next, visually review these two samples. Once their direction is accepted, create the full muscle set on a consistent 3:4 canvas and recreate the equipment reference photographs at the agreed resolution. Each production batch must have a new immutable manifest/cache version and exact-SHA browser validation before it is used by the app.

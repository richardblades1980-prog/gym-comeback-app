## Proposed catalogue representation

1. **Definition:** stable opaque ID, canonical display name, status, provenance and reviewed primary/secondary muscle references.
2. **Variation:** separate stable ID linked to a definition where the movement is genuinely a variation (e.g. equipment, body position, angle or grip); do not collapse meaningful distinctions.
3. **Equipment requirements:** structured equipment IDs plus source labels/accepted gear terms. The current free-text gear-match terms are not exercise aliases.
4. **Instructions and safety cues:** explicit, reviewed exercise-specific fields. Mark missing instructions as incomplete; do not publish the generic fallback as a specific how-to guide.
5. **Artwork references:** local asset IDs/paths with existence and offline-precache checks. Equipment illustration is illustrative, not proof that a specific machine model is equivalent.
6. **Programme slots:** preserve day, slot order, source label, prescription/configuration and pinned catalogue release. Substitutions/swaps remain explicit slot-level decisions.
7. **Provenance:** source file/blob, exact legacy label, source fields, review decision, reviewer and release. Do not silently rewrite historical labels.

Choose a plan length. The 8- and 12-week options extend the starter block with gradual progression and easier consolidation weeks; they are not a promise of results.

How longer plans work
Weeks 1–4 establish technique and tolerance. Weeks 5–8 build gradually. Weeks 9–12 continue only if recovery is good. Every week still has two logged workouts, with optional additional days guided by your profile. Reduce load or repeat a week when needed.

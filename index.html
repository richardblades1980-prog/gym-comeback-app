Exercise logging display

Off by default. Weight fields are hidden for no-weight movements, but can be shown whenever you need to record added resistance. Your existing logs are preserved.

How longer plans work
Weeks 1–4 establish technique and tolerance. Weeks 5–8 build gradually. Weeks 9–12 continue only if recovery is good. Choose 2–5 training days per week in your profile. Days 1–5 have separate workout plans and logs; only schedule as many days as you can recover from. Reduce load or repeat a week when needed.

Backups & privacy

Your logs and injury profile stay in local browser storage on this device. They are not synced to other devices. Download a JSON backup regularly and keep a copy somewhere safe. A backup file contains personal training and profile details, so store it privately. Anyone with access to this browser profile may be able to see saved entries. Restoring a backup replaces matching workout records.
